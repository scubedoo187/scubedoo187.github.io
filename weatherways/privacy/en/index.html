WeatherWays Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

WeatherWays (the "App") is a mobile game provided by independent developer 정영균 (Joung Yeonggyun). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or contact details. The following information may be collected automatically through third-party SDKs.

InformationCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)Google AdMobServing rewarded ads, preventing ad fraud, measuring ad performance
Device information (model, OS version, language), approximate location (country based on IP address), app interactions, diagnostics (crashes)Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase CrashlyticsServing ads, analyzing app stability, usage statistics
Purchase history (product ID, purchase token)Google Play BillingProcessing and restoring in-app purchases
Anonymous authentication ID (random user ID), random nickname generated on the device, best scores and play statistics per city and difficultyFirebase Authentication (anonymous), Cloud FirestoreScoreboard (showing your rank and top records). No account information such as name or email is used
Game progress data (scores, unlock status, settings)Stored on the deviceSaving game progress. Not sent to the developer's servers

2. Use and Sharing of Information

  • Collected information is used only to serve ads, improve app stability, and analyze usage statistics.
  • The developer does not store or sell personally identifiable information.
  • Third-party SDK policies: Google Privacy Policy, How AdMob uses data, Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Advertising

The App uses rewarded ads from Google AdMob. You can choose whether to watch ads, and you can opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Purchasing the full bundle or the ad-removal product removes the need to watch ads.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

How to request deletion of your WeatherWays data (developer: Joung Yeonggyun)

  1. Email scubedoo187@gmail.com with the subject "WeatherWays data deletion request".
  2. Include your in-game nickname (Settings → Nickname).
  3. Within 30 days of receiving your request we delete your scoreboard records (anonymous ID, nickname, scores) and reply. No other data is retained by us. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and cannot be deleted by the developer.
  • Game progress data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Scoreboard records (anonymous ID, nickname, scores) are stored in Firebase. Uninstalling the App breaks the link to your anonymous ID; to have your records deleted, contact us at the address below with your nickname and we will process the request.
  • Retention of analytics and advertising data collected by third-party SDKs is governed by each provider's policies. You may send deletion requests to the contact below, and we will handle them to the extent possible.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
